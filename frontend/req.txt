Product description

STUDENT

Screen 1: Student Dashboard (Home)
Purpose: Central hub showing the student's current academic health at a glance
Sections / Components:
Header Banner
Student name, profile photo, roll number, current level (Foundation / Diploma / Degree)
Current term indicator (e.g., "Term 5 of 9")
A Risk Status Badge — color coded: 🟢 Safe / 🟡 At Risk / 🔴 Critical
Clicking this badge opens a drawer/modal explaining why they have that status and what to do
Milestone Progress Card
Visual progress bar: "X of 8 Foundation courses completed"
Sub-indicators:
"4 courses in 4 terms" checkpoint — ✅ Met / ⚠️ Approaching / ❌ Missed
"6 courses in 6 terms" checkpoint — same
"8 courses in 9 terms" final deadline — countdown in terms remaining
If a checkpoint is missed → card turns red with a CTA: "See what this means for you"
Quick Stats Row (4 stat cards side by side)
Courses Completed
Courses In Progress (this term)
Assignments Pending
Quizzes Pending
Assigned TA Card
TA name, photo, contact button (in-app message trigger)
If no TA assigned: "No TA assigned yet" with a muted state
Trigger: populated automatically when Admin assigns a TA
Recent Notifications Strip
Last 3 unread notifications with timestamps
"View All" link → goes to Notifications Screen
Upcoming Deadlines Widget
List of next 5 deadlines (assignments, quizzes, exams) sorted by date
Each item: Course name, task name, due date, days remaining
Color coded: green (>7 days), yellow (3–7 days), red (<3 days)

Screen 2: Course Planner
Purpose: Helps students plan which courses to take in upcoming terms based on prerequisites, current status, and milestone requirements
Sections / Components:
Term-wise Grid View
Columns = Terms (Term 1 through Term 9)
Rows = Courses
Each cell shows: Completed ✅ / In Progress 🔄 / Registered / Not Started / Blocked (due to prerequisite)
Blocked courses show a lock icon with tooltip: "Requires [Course X] to be completed first"
Prerequisite Chain Visualizer
Toggle button: "Show Prerequisite Map"
On toggle: a tree/flow diagram showing which courses unlock which
Highlight the student's current position in the chain
Highlight any "bottleneck" courses (e.g., Intro to Python OPPE) in orange
Course Registration Status Panel
This term's registered courses
Available courses for next term (based on prerequisites cleared)
Warning if student hasn't registered for enough courses to meet milestone pace
e.g., "You need to register for at least 2 more courses this term to stay on track"
Feasibility Indicator
AI-generated assessment: "Based on your current pace, you are [on track / at risk of missing] the 8-course milestone"
Shows projected completion term for remaining courses
If infeasible: shows "You cannot complete all 8 courses in 9 terms at your current pace. Here's what to do →"
Actions
"Request Advisor Review" → triggers mentorship request flow (see Screen 5)

Screen 3: My Progress
Purpose: Detailed academic history and performance across all courses
Sections / Components:
Course History Table
Columns: Course Name | Term | Grade | Attempts | Status (Pass/Fail/Ongoing/Dropped)
Sortable and filterable by term, status, grade
Repeat attempts highlighted — e.g., "Attempt 2 of 3" shown in orange
Performance Trend Chart
Line chart: Average grade per term over time
Overlay: Milestone checkpoints marked on the x-axis
Assignment & Quiz Tracker
Per-course breakdown:
Assignments: submitted / total, avg score
Quizzes: attempted / total, avg score
OPPE status per course (if applicable)
Repeat Course Section
Dedicated section showing courses attempted more than once
For each: shows attempt history, scores, current status
If repeated 2+ times → shows warning: "Multiple attempts detected. Consider reaching out to your TA."

Screen 4: Notifications
Purpose: Centralized notification center for all alerts relevant to the student
Notification Types & Triggers:
Risk Alert
Trigger: AI pipeline flags the student as at-risk or upgrades risk level
Content: "You have been flagged as At Risk. Reason: [X]. Recommended action: [Y]"
Action button: "View Details" → opens risk explanation modal
Action button: "Request Mentorship" → shortcut to Screen 5
TA Assignment Notification
Trigger: Admin assigns a TA to the student
Content: "[TA Name] has been assigned as your Teaching Assistant. Tap to view their profile."
Action: Opens TA profile card
Missing Assignment / Quiz Alert
Trigger: Assignment or quiz deadline passes without submission
Content: "You missed [Assignment Name] in [Course]. This may affect your standing."
Frequency: Sent once at deadline, then once more 24 hours after if still unsubmitted
Milestone Warning
Trigger: Student is on pace to miss a checkpoint (calculated at start of each term)
Content: "At your current rate, you may not meet the 4-course-in-4-term milestone. Here's what you can do."
Action button: "View Course Planner"
Mentorship Request Status
Trigger: Admin accepts or declines a mentorship request
Content: "Your mentorship request has been [accepted/reviewed]. [Message from admin if any]"
UI Details:
Each notification has: icon (type), title, short description, timestamp, read/unread state
Unread shown with a dot indicator
"Mark all as read" button at top
Filter tabs: All / Risk / Academic / TA / System
Infinite scroll or pagination

Screen 5: Mentorship Request
Purpose: Student formally requests help/mentorship from Admin
Form Fields:
Subject (dropdown): Academic Difficulty / Course Planning / Personal Circumstances / OPPE Help / Other
Description (text area): "Describe what you need help with" (max 500 chars)
Urgency level (toggle): Normal / Urgent
Attach relevant course(s): multi-select dropdown from their course list
State Management:
After submission: shows "Request Submitted" confirmation with expected response time
If a request is already pending: shows status tracker (Submitted → Reviewed → Responded)
History of past requests viewable below the form
If Admin responds: notification triggers + response visible here

Screen 6: Profile & Settings
Personal details (read-only from system data)
Notification preferences (toggle per notification type: In-App / Email)
Change password
Linked TA (read-only)

Problem statement
The IITM BS Degree Program has students progressing through different levels of the program: Foundation, Diploma and Degree. At present, it is difficult for program administrators to systematically identify students who may be at risk of academic disengagement, delayed progression or dropping out.
The goal of this project is to design and develop a platform that can help identify potentially at-risk students at different stages of the program, notify relevant administrators and support appropriate interventions.
The system should enable relevant stakeholders to understand why a student may be at risk, track interventions, and provide appropriate recommendations or next steps for students, faculty and administrators.
Expected outcome
An API-based platform that can:
Identify and categorize students who may require attention based on relevant indicators.
Provide appropriate information and alerts to TAs/administrators.
Support tracking of interventions and their outcomes.
Suggest possible interventions or remedial actions for different stakeholders.
Provide appropriate views and functionality for students, faculty/TAs and administrators.
Maintain a history of important decisions/actions so that interventions can be followed up.
Teams are expected to determine the relevant indicators, workflows, user roles and intervention mechanisms through requirements analysis and interaction with the client.
